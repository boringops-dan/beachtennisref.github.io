# beachtennisref.github.io

Static marketing site for [BeachTennisRef.App](https://beachtennisref.app) -- a beach tennis scorekeeping / referee app with automatic ITF rule enforcement. Sibling of `volleyref.github.io` (the volleyball marketing site); same stack and conventions, beach-tennis brand.

## Architecture

- Standalone HTML pages (no template system, no SSG). Each page is self-contained.
- Shared CSS in `css/style.css`, shared JS in `js/` directory.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages, served at beachtennisref.app via custom domain.

## Analytics

- **Marketing site GA4:** `G-JELDXQYBLN` -- the dedicated GA4 property for beachtennisref.app (do NOT reuse the volleyref property `G-MRGTZX69JM`). Tag is in `<head>` on every page; event tracking in `js/analytics.js`.
- **Web app GA4:** tracked separately in the app repo (`web_referee`); the BT app shares the multi-sport build.

- **App / engine:** lives in `../VolleyballReferee2/web_referee` (beach tennis is the `packages/beach-tennis` sport). This repo is marketing only -- no app code.

## Legal pages: English is the book of record

- `terms.html`, `privacy.html` and `delete-account.html` are hand-written in English and are the only legally binding versions (each terms/privacy page says so in its "Language" section).
- Spanish (`es/`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`pt/`) versions are GENERATED by `scripts/legal-translations.mjs` from `_legal/<locale>/<page>` (front matter + translated `.legal-prose` body) and `_legal/<locale>/chrome.json` (nav/footer strings, the "English prevails" notice). Never hand-edit `es/` or `pt/`; never hand-edit the `legal-i18n:*` marker regions in the English pages.
- Each translation records `source-sha`, the fingerprint of the English text it was translated from. Any change to an English legal page's title, "Last updated" line or body makes `node scripts/validate-site.mjs` (the deploy gate) fail until every translation of that page is redone and its `source-sha` updated to the value the error prints. Reindenting English is not a change.
- After editing English or `_legal/`, run `node scripts/legal-translations.mjs` and commit its output. `node --test scripts/legal-translations.test.mjs` covers the generator.
- Glossary and voice follow the app's (`../VolleyballReferee2/markdown/docs/I18N_GLOSSARY.md`): neutral Latin American Spanish with `tú`, Brazilian Portuguese with `você`. Legal link labels match the app catalogs ("Términos de uso", "Termos de Serviço").
- `scripts/legal-translations.mjs` and its test are byte-identical across volleyref.github.io and beachtennisref.github.io; mirror any edit.

## App Store Connect sync

- `terms.txt` is the plain-text mirror of `terms.html` pasted into App Store Connect's Custom License Agreement field (App Information > License Agreement > Edit), which only accepts plain text. It is generated from `terms.html` by `scripts/legal-translations.mjs` and checked by the validator, so it can no longer drift from the page.
- Whenever `terms.html` changes, paste the regenerated `terms.txt` into App Store Connect for BeachTennisRef. The English text is the only one that goes there. (The two once drifted: Feb 2026 EULA text stayed in ASC after the site moved to Aug 2026 terms, and only an Apple rejection surfaced it.)

## Conventions

- Never use em dashes in copy or code.
- All pages should have: meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph tags, Twitter Card tags, structured data where appropriate.
- New content pages must be added to `sitemap.xml` and linked from the footer.
- Brand: "Beach Tennis Referee" / BeachTennisRef.App; CTAs point at `https://app.beachtennisref.app`. Never volleyball branding or volleyref.app links.
