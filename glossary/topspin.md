# Topspin - Beach Tennis Glossary

> Topspin is forward rotation that makes the ball dip downward in flight. A ball with topspin would bounce forward aggressively if it were allowed to bounce. In beach tennis, where the ball cannot bounce, topspin is used to bring powerful drives and groundstrokes safely over the net while aiming them deep. The topspin drive is a primary offensive shot in beach tennis, often used to attack a high ball or a loose volley from the opponent.

**Ruleset:** ITF
**Rule Reference:** ITF Beach Tennis Rules, Rule 9 (The Ball in Play)

**Related Terms:** backspin, drive, spin-serve
